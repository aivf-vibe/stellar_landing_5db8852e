Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TheraConnect

Is TheraConnect HIPAA compliant?

Absolutely. TheraConnect is fully HIPAA compliant with bank-level encryption, secure data storage, and regular security audits. We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with all our customers.

Can I import my existing client data?

Yes! We provide easy data migration tools and our support team will help you import your existing client information from spreadsheets, other CRMs, or paper records securely.

Do you offer training and support?

We provide comprehensive onboarding, video tutorials, and live training sessions. All plans include email support, with priority support and dedicated account management for higher tiers.

Can I use TheraConnect on mobile devices?

Yes! TheraConnect works seamlessly on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile. We also offer dedicated mobile apps for iOS and Android for on-the-go practice management.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You own your data. If you decide to leave, we'll help you export all your client information in standard formats. We also maintain secure backups for 30 days after cancellation.

Do you integrate with insurance companies?

Yes! We integrate with major insurance providers for eligibility verification, claims submission, and payment processing. We're constantly adding new insurance integrations.
